# agora — code audit + repair, 2026-10-12 (1.8.1)

> **Scope**: a full-tree audit of agora 1.8.0 (`cdd49c5`), no new features. Six parallel slices, each
> told to verify by repro rather than by reading: **the wire** (`telnet.cyr`, `process_rx`, the fuzz
> harness); **storage and identity** (`board.cyr`, `account.cyr`, the CLI verbs, login); **serve loops and
> memory** (both serve models, the session pool, the send path, signals, the ADR 0021/0022 allocator
> gates including allocations inside vendored stdlib functions); **door framework + trade/strategy doors**
> (`door.cyr`, Smuggler, Port Authority, The Handler, wager); **RPG/sim/war doors, chat, chatbots and the
> Descent gateway**; and **closeout gates + doc-vs-code truth** (dead code, deferrals, stale comments,
> CI). Every finding below was re-verified by the lead before it was fixed, and every fix was A/B'd
> against a `git archive` build of 1.8.0 using the auditors' own repro scripts.
>
> **Result**: **3 HIGH, 12 MEDIUM, 26 LOW** fixed; **14 INFO** recorded, with the open ones scheduled in
> [`roadmap.md`](../development/roadmap.md). No CRITICAL.
>
> **Disposition — CLOSED in 1.8.1** for every HIGH, MEDIUM and LOW. Two LOWs are mitigated rather than
> cured, and say so (L19, and the agnos half of M12).

Previous audit: [2026-10-12](2026-10-12-audit.md) (1.8.0, closed in the same cut). Severity follows
CLAUDE.md § Security Hardening. **Damaged-file findings** (a save, a world snapshot, a transcript, a
leaderboard) need write access to the server-owned store, which already grants everything else; they are
rated by their effect on OTHER sessions — an out-of-bounds **write** into the shared poll process is
MEDIUM, a bounded read or a clean truncation is LOW. (1.4.4 rated one such write HIGH; this audit keeps
the precondition visible instead.)

## HIGH

### H1. Unauthenticated, pipelinable heap growth from vendored allocations on per-line paths
`src/board.cyr` `post_max_id` / `post_list` / `boards_list` / `board_exists`, `src/account.cyr`
`account_resolve_handle` / `parse_auth_sig`, `post_format`.
(Serve P1, storage S11, wire W2, hygiene H20.)

The ADR 0021 arena reclaims agora's own per-command scratch, but not allocations made **inside stdlib
functions** agora calls per line: `dir_list` (a vec + one `Str` per entry, ~170 B per call + ~75 B per
entry), `str_from` (16 B), sigil's `hex_decode` (64 B per `auth:`), chrono's `iso8601_now` (72 B per post).
`login x` scans `.users/` and `list` / `read` / `boards` scan boards, all with no login, nine bytes per
pipelined command. Measured on 1.8.0 under poll from ONE anonymous connection: **+2.5 MB/s on an empty
store, +15 MB/s with 301 users** — a fresh BBS to OOM in minutes, taking all 64 sessions (on agnos, the
only model). The roadmap carried the `dir_list` residue as a medium-effort cleanup with no severity.

**Fixed**: one process-lifetime listing workspace over `dir_list_into` (allocates nothing; grows only by
doubling, so bounded by the largest directory, not by calls); `path_str` builds `Str` headers in the
arena; `hex_decode_into` decodes straight into the caller's buffer; `iso8601_into` formats the Date
header with no allocation (byte-identical to chrono's, t229). Per-command growth, 1.8.0 → 1.8.1, poll,
2,000 reps: `login nobody` **223 → 0 B**, `login qix` + `auth:` **287 → 0 B**, `enter <board>` **16 → 0 B**,
authenticated `post` on a growing board **174–315 KB → ~0**. Also closes the half of 2026-07-26 H2's
"login path" leak the 1.6.3 arena could not see. t228, t229.

### H2. One anonymous client could stall every session under poll
`src/main.cyr` `process_rx`, `serve_poll` sweep; `src/board.cyr` `replies_to`. (Wire W1, storage S3.)

`process_rx` ran every complete line of a 4 KB recv before the sweep moved on, and `replies_to` read up to
66 KB of every post on every `read`. One packet of `read 1\r` is 585 commands. **Repro** (200 posts × 6 KB,
victim echo latency): baseline 10 ms; 4 anonymous attackers → **3,587 ms**; 16 → **13,701 ms**. `list` and
`login x` floods do the same.

**Fixed**: a per-sweep line budget (`POLL_LINE_BUDGET` = 1 counted line per session per pass); the rest of
the recv is parked in the slot (`SESS_RX_OFF`/`LEN`) and runs first next sweep; the sweep skips its sleep
while anything is parked, so a lone scripted client keeps full speed. Compose lines (subject/body) don't
count, so a pasted post is not throttled. `replies_to` now reads a 2 KB header scan per post. 16 attackers
→ **22 ms** (`read`), **23 ms** (`list`, was 1,077), **19 ms** (`login`, was 200). Smoke 30 check E.

### H3. Per-iteration arena allocations spilled the arena — ~400 KB leaked per `list` or `read`
`src/board.cyr` `post_read`, `src/account.cyr` `post_from`, `account_lookup_handle`. **Found by the lead
while re-measuring H1.**

`post_read` took its 512-byte path from `cmd_alloc`, and `list` and `replies_to` call it once **per post**
(`post_from`, ~64 B, likewise per post; `account_lookup_handle`, 512 B, once per user in the handle scan).
On a ~1,500-post board that is ~770 KB in one command, past the 512 KB arena, after which **every**
`cmd_alloc` in that command silently falls back to a never-reclaimed bare `alloc()`: **~400 KB per
anonymous `list` or `read`**. 1.8.0 hid it — a `list` that size overflowed the tx queue (M8) and the
session was dropped first; measuring `read` there showed 95 KB/command before the session died.

**Fixed**: all three use fixed-size stack buffers. `list` and `read` on a 9,751-post board: **0 B per
command** (2,000 reps). t232 calls all three 3,000 times inside one "command" and asserts the arena never
moves; mutation-checked (restoring the `cmd_alloc` in `post_read` turns it red). The lesson is now a
Closeout Pass gate (CLAUDE.md).

## MEDIUM

### M1. Door save lifecycle: disconnecting undid costs, finished runs replayed forever
`src/main.cyr` `door_save_on_exit`, the `play` launcher, every close path. (Doors D1, D2, G3; serve INFO.)

A player's state was saved **only on a clean `q`**, while a Universe world was written on every line.
- **Disconnect to undo**: deploy fighters, drop the link, re-enter — the garrison grew 29 → 58 → 87 → 116
  → 145 while the ship kept its 30; bet all-in and drop on a loss — Port Authority credits **2,000 →
  893,641** in 14 bets, Olympiad **700 → 2,921** denarii, any loss erased. Handler accusations and decrypts
  became free (breaking ADR 0019 rule 2). Two sessions under one identity gave the same result.
- **Finished runs never closed**: a finished Universe run was saved finished and re-entered straight onto
  the end screen, posting again each time (6 rows from 1 finish) and locking the player out; a finished
  Solo run left its last checkpoint on disk, replayable to the leaderboard indefinitely (Decode: one
  answer, three "Codebreaker" rows).

**Fixed**: every close path — poll close, poll shutdown, the fork child's EOF — checkpoints exactly like
`q` (`session_door_checkpoint`); a finished run **removes** its save; a save that loads already over is
discarded for a fresh run; a run that was already over at entry (Ashes domination) does not post again;
and a **per-identity, per-game play lock** (`<game>.lock` beside the save, `flock` non-blocking) refuses a
second session. Repros: garrison stays 29; losses stick (0 credits after the losses); one leaderboard row.
Smoke 30 checks G and H, under both serve models.

### M2. Save and world loaders trusted index fields → out-of-bounds heap writes
`port_authority.cyr` `pa_load_parse` + `paw_*`; `handler.cyr` `th_load_parse` + `thw_*`; `smuggler.cyr`
`slw_*`; `decode.cyr` `decode_load_parse`; `ashes.cyr` `ashw_valid`. (Doors D3, G9, G10; hygiene H12.)

Counts were clamped (1.4.4) but not what they index: `PA_SECTOR`, `PA_PLANET`, `TH_MOLEIDX`, `AG_CITY`,
cable agent/city fields, Decode's `MAXTRIES` (and `TRIES` clamped inclusive), Ashes' `norders`. Results:
a garrison deploy wrote the player's fp and a chosen fighter count past the session slot's world buffer;
`AG_CITY = 4` wrote past the alert snapshot; `PA_SECTOR = 1e12` crashed a poll server outright; a Decode
guess wrote row 10 past an 80-byte freelist block; a negative Ashes order count wrote 32 bytes before the
snapshot. Precondition: a damaged, tampered or older-format file.

**Fixed**: loaders reject out-of-range index fields (the launcher sets the save aside — M1); the shared-
world accessors refuse out-of-range cells; Decode clamps to the buffers it allocated and rejects a live
game with no tries left; `ashw_valid` checks the order count, order provinces and pact statuses. t230.

### M3. `.policy` failed OPEN
`src/board.cyr` `board_policy_get`. (Storage S1.)

Any read error and any text that was not exactly `open`/`known`/`admin` meant **open**: `admin # ops
only`, `Admin`, a BOM, a leading space, a root-owned 0600 file, a directory named `.policy` — all seven
variants admitted a registered non-admin to an admin-only board.

**Fixed**: only a **missing** file is open (ADR 0006). The deciding token is the first line left after a
`#` comment is cut and whitespace trimmed; anything unrecognised or unreadable is **admin-only**, with a
stderr warning. `store_read_missing` keeps agnos working (its errors are all -1). t226; smoke 30 check A.

### M4. `.admins` past 4 KB: the cut line matched as a handle
`src/board.cyr` `board_admin_check`. (Storage S2 — worse than the backlog row it shared a line with.)

The read stopped at 4,096 bytes and the scanner treated end-of-buffer as end-of-line, so `qixbert`
straddling the cut admitted `qix`. **Fixed**: only whole lines inside the cap are trusted
(`board_admins_whole`), with a warning. t227; smoke 30 check B.

### M5. Every Handler decrypt leaked a Decode state — reachable without login
`src/handler.cyr` `th_feed_decrypt`, `th_free`. (Doors D4, serve P2, hygiene H21.)

`th_begin_decrypt` builds a five-buffer Decode state; nothing freed it. ~738 B per attempt, linear, in
practice mode. **Fixed**: freed at the end of the round and in `th_free`. 735 → **0 B** per crack cycle.

### M6. Every `descent` leaked ~650 B of bare heap
`src/descent.cyr` `descent_run`, `descent_proxy`. (Doors G1, serve P6, hygiene H20.)

Four bare `alloc()`s per command, even on "No Descent linked."; the proxy's 4 KB buffer per visit too.
CLAUDE.md's exception covered only the proxy buffers. **Fixed**: arena (`cmd_alloc`) throughout; the
CLAUDE.md exception is retired.

### M7. Under poll, a player was disconnected the moment they returned from the MUD
`src/main.cyr` (`descent` command), `src/descent.cyr`. (Doors G2.)

`SESS_LAST_MS` was not refreshed during the visit, so any visit over 60 s ended with "-- back at the agora
--" and an immediate reap. **Fixed**: `session_after_descent` refreshes it.

### M8. Under poll, any command printing > 128 KB dropped the session
`src/main.cyr` `sess_tx_enqueue`. (Storage S5, serve P3.)

The queue drains only after a command returns, so "full" meant "big command", not "slow reader": a
`list` of ~600+ posts cut at 131,072 B and closed — on agnos, any busy board unlistable. **Fixed**: drain
and compact before declaring overflow; a peer that has really stopped reading still overflows. A 168 KB
`list`: **131,072 B + close → 168,470 B, session open**. Smoke 30 check F.

### M9. Fork mode had no connection cap, and IAC NOP held a child forever
`src/main.cyr` fork accept loop, `handle_client`. (Serve P4.)

200 idle connections → 200 processes, unbounded to `RLIMIT_NPROC`; `SO_RCVTIMEO` restarted on any byte,
so a 2-byte keepalive every 20 s outlived the 60 s bound. **Fixed**: live children are counted and capped
at `MAX_SESS` ("server full"), and the fork worker applies the 1.6.4 data-only idle rule. 200 → **64**
children; the keepalive session is reaped like a silent one.

### M10. Fork mode: a non-reading client pinned its child for hours
`src/main.cyr` `send_buf`. (Hygiene H19, serve P10.)

`session_execute` discards almost every send result, and each write to a non-reading client waits the
full 60 s `SO_SNDTIMEO`: `read 1` × 500 kept the child alive past 195 s (theoretical bound: hours).
**Fixed**: a failed fork-mode send is sticky (`SESS_TX_OVF`, as poll's queue already was) — later sends
return at once and `process_rx` closes the session.

*1.8.2 follow-up*: the latch fired only on a write that gained no bytes — a SHORT (timed-out) write still
counted as success and was retried, and on a zero-window TCP peer the kernel can take a few KB more on a
probe's ACK, so the child lived 186 s (three timeouts) rather than 60. A short write now latches too
(CHANGELOG [1.8.2] § Fixed; `tests/serve.tcyr` s07; smoke 31).

### M11. The 1.6.4 idle-reap fix could be bypassed with one NUL byte
`src/main.cyr` `process_rx`, both serve loops. (Serve P9, wire W2, doors G11.)

The activity refresh ran **before** the input filter, so a dropped NUL every 50 s held a slot exactly like
the IAC NOP 1.6.4 closed; the chat reaper's counter reset on any recv. **Fixed**: both move only on a byte
the session keeps.

### M12. agnos: the "one send per drain" bound silently broke at the 1.8.0 pin move
`src/main.cyr` `session_drain`. (Hygiene H5 — verified by reading the vendored stdlib; not run on agnos.)

Since cyrius 6.6.7 the agnos `sys_write` on a socket is `_agnos_sock_send_all`, which retries a 0 until a
30 s stall deadline re-armed on any progress: one zero-window peer froze the whole agnos sweep for 30 s+,
a trickling peer indefinitely. **Fixed**: one raw `sock_send#48` per drain, restoring ADR 0023's bound
(one send, which the kernel may itself hold ~8 s, then yield).
